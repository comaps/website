We release map updates roughly once a week. This pace is possible because map updates are no longer tied to app updates since May 2026. A basic recent map update will ship with each app update, but you are also able to [check for updates](@/support/how-do-i-ensure-that-i-have-the-latest-app-version-and-map-data/index.md) in the download screen and get updated maps until the app version is too old for compatibility with new maps.
