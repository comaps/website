Some of the highlights of this release are:

- Improved turn instructions for roundabouts, U-turns and other cases by @bluelight_henry
- Turn-by-turn navigation directions preview in route planning by @LMBishop
- Improved highway exit number in next turn instructions by @matheusgomesms
- Added road shields to next turn direction signs by @AndrewShkrob via OM, @LMBishop, @matheusgomesms, and @eisa01
- iOS: Lane guidance and improved next turn descriptions by @eisa01
- iOS: Added exit number to the roundabout directions sign in CarPlay by @eisa01
- iOS: Show turn instructions in the car instrument cluster in CarPlay by @eisa01
- Android: Separate routing options per transport mode by @nilshasler
- Android: Added destination and intermediate stop icons to navigation progress bar by @gpesquero
- Android: Search across all favourites lists by by @marko
- OpenStreetMap data as of July 14 and further weekly updates

...and many other improvements, see [the full list on Codeberg](https://codeberg.org/comaps/comaps/releases/tag/v2026.07.16-10)!
