> *CoMaps is a fairly new offline map app that is ideal for international travel, hiking, and travelling to remote areas. It is powered by community-driven OpenStreetMap data, providing extremely detailed and community-verified map data, footpaths, local trails, and extensive points of interest (POIs). It is a fantastic tool to use if you have a cap on your cellular data plan or are going to places with limited connectivity.*
