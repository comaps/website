CoMaps contributors Anton Wenemoser and Bastian Greshake Tzovaras spoke with the
Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team about how CoMaps was used during the earthquake 
response in Venezuela.

> On the second day after the earthquake, Anton went down to what everyone had 
> started calling ground zero, the hardest-hit area. He'd been helping in 
> Caracas before, but now he was heading into a zone with no cell coverage. 
> He didn't have to improvise: he already had the app on his phone.
